LQ 燕云 Logs capture engine

The protocol helpers under tools/ are adapted from the user-supplied
YyslsLogs 0.2.1 local distribution (resources/app/package.json declares
GPL-3.0-or-later). Original authorship and existing comments are retained.
The session supervisor, event publication, archive integration and desktop
integration were adapted for LQ-yysls-logs. Distributed under GPL-3.0-or-later.

Third-party runtime licenses remain next to their components:
Node.js, Python, Wireshark, protobuf and @msgpack/msgpack.
Node.js v24.19.0 license source: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/nodejs/node/v24.19.0/LICENSE
Npcap is an external installed prerequisite, not redistributed by this project.
Protocol descriptors and property maps are locally imported compatibility
assets. Raw sample sessions and connection secrets are not redistributed.
Current field mappings are version-specific and do not establish complete
team state, exact server execution timing, or validated rDPS.
